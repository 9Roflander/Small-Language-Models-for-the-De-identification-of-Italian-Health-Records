Phase 2 — Distribution Fix

The NOME over-representation problem

Style was fixed, but the model still hallucinated [NOME] tags everywhere. The cause: the synthetic data had 4× more names per note than the gold standard.

4× over-representation of names in synthetic data.

What this caused

The model began hallucinating [NOME] tags in normal text, crushing precision.

The fix: name-free generator

A second generator (generate_noname.py) instructs Gemini to refer to patients only through anonymous descriptors:

la paziente l'uomo il soggetto il paziente

Any output containing a NOME entity is hard-rejected by the validator.
