Conclusion

Three findings that stand independent of the headline number

1

Style transfer matters more than volume

Mismatched synthetic data gave F1 = 0.18 — worse than zero-shot. Getting the style right (~$10 in Gemini API) was the single largest improvement in the project.

2

Entity distribution must be controlled

4× NOME over-representation in synthetic data collapsed NOME precision. Adding 475 name-free examples moved NOME F1 from 0.36 → 0.71. The effect is predictable and fixable.

3

More style-matched data reduces variance

Doubling from 878 → 1,676 synthetic samples halved CV std (0.144 → 0.073). Style-matched volume has diminishing mean returns but continuing variance returns past 1,700 samples.

Headline result

A 3B fine-tuned model beats the best 12B zero-shot model from Miranda et al. (CLiC-it 2025) by +0.029 macro-F1 using a single 12 GB GPU. A 1B model nearly matches it.

Fine-tuning with style-anchored synthetic data closes the scale gap on narrow, well-defined NLP tasks.
