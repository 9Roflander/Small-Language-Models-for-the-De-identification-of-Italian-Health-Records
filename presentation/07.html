Phase 2 — Critical Failure

The style disaster: mismatched synthetic data

The first synthetic dataset (no style reference) produced a catastrophic result.

0.18
Macro-F1 on mismatched
synthetic training data

Worse than the smallest zero-shot baseline (0.41).
Fine-tuning made things actively worse.

What the model actually learned

It learned a shortcut classifier:

Synthetic-style text → redact it
Real clinical text (gold style) → leave it alone

Gold notes looked real, so the model left them untouched.

The most important finding in this project:

Mismatched synthetic data does not just fail to help — it actively degrades the model. Training on the wrong style is worse than no training at all.
